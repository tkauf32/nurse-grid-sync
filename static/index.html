How to Find Your NurseGrid URL

  1. Login to NurseGrid on a computer web browser.
  2. Click on the "Calendar" tab on the left side.
  3. Click the "Options" button on the right side.
  4. Enable "Calendar Sharing".
  5. Copy your Calendar URL shown under “Your Calendar URL”.
